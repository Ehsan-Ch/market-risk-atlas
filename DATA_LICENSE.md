# Data attribution

Source: Binance Vision ETHUSDT spot aggregate trades,1–14January2024. Exact URLs, original publisher checksum URLs and SHA256 pins are in `data/source_manifest.json`. The archives were originally downloaded for [TickLab](https://github.com/Ehsan-Ch/tick-calibration-lab) on30October2026 and reverified locally for this study on17November2026.

The [provider's dataset terms](https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data/blob/master/TERMS_AND_CONDITIONS.md), version1.0 dated26August2026, identify **CC BY-NC-SA4.0** licensing and additional provider terms. Consult them before reuse. This project is non-monetized educational research and provides no live trading service.

Raw archives are excluded from Git. This project transforms source trades into five-minute returns, normalized price statistics, volatility and downside-risk forecasts.

07 / READ THE ASSUMPTIONS

Simple enough to inspect.

Historical VaR uses the previous 864 returns. EWMA updates variance after each observed return, with decay 0.94 and a zero-mean Gaussian tail assumption.

The decay is an illustrative five-minute setting, not an optimized parameter or a daily RiskMetrics replication. Study parameters were fixed before evaluating outcomes. One venue, one asset and two weeks cannot establish future reliability.
